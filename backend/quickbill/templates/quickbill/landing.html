7 Core Modules of a Professional POS

Seamlessly integrates all departments of grocery stores, supermarkets, and retail counters into one unified system:

1. Sales & Checkout

Lightning-fast barcode scanning, natural language text bulk entry, thermal printing, and multi-payment processing.

  • Hardware Barcode Scanner & 3D Touch Quick Keys
  • Free-text bulk natural item parsing with instant auto-calc
  • Discounts, round-off, and customer refund processing
  • Split payments (Cash, Card, Digital Khata Credit)
02
📦

2. Inventory & Stock

Real-time stock deduction, low stock alert notifications, batch numbers, and expiry date monitoring.

  • Live stock quantity tracking with automated deduction
  • Automated low stock threshold warnings
  • Expiry dates tracking with 7-day alert badges
  • Damaged/spoilage stock write-offs and physical audit logs
03
🚚

3. Purchases & Suppliers

Vendor purchasing, PO invoice recording, stock receiving, supplier payments, and accounts payable.

  • Complete supplier contact directory & profile management
  • Direct PO bill entry with automatic stock updates
  • Cost price tracking and margin calculations
  • Supplier payments ledger & accounts payable balance
04
👥

4. Customers & Khata Ledger

Digital customer credit ledger, credit limits, automated debt recovery reminders, and loyalty rewards.

  • Customer profiles with complete purchase history
  • Digital Khata credit ledger with WhatsApp balance alerts
  • Configurable credit limits to prevent unpaid risk
  • Customer loyalty points and rewards tracking
05
💰

5. Accounts & Expenses

Daily operational expenses, cash drawer shift sessions, cash float control, and net profit tracking.

  • Expense vouchers (Rent, Utilities/DEWA, Salaries, Tea)
  • Shift opening cash float & end-of-shift closing drawer audits
  • Direct supplier cash payout logging
  • Net cash flow and profit & loss (P&L) statements
06
📊

6. Reports & Analytics

Daily, weekly, and monthly sales performance, top best-selling items, and cashier shift audits.

  • Gross revenue, cash vs credit ratios, and net margins
  • Top 10 Best-Selling Grocery Items
  • Automated gross margin and net profit calculation
  • Staff & cashier sales performance metrics

🔐

7. Users & Role Permissions

Role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Cashier) with fast 4-digit PIN authentication.

👑 Owner / Admin Full access: profit reports, product deletion, supplier rates, and staff settings
👔 Store Manager Stock In, purchase orders, supplier payments, customer khata, and refund approval
💳 Cashier Fast checkout & cash/card collection only; restricted from profits and deletions
